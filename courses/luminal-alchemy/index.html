Luminal Alchemy

Sacred Geometry is the code of creation, a testimony of the manifest universe, originating from 7 divine matrices.

Through Luminal Alchemy, you stop being a passive observer and become a conscious operator of these matrices of light, transmuting ignorance into the gold of spiritual wisdom.
It is the transition from the light of truth to practical action in matter.

Master Sacred Geometry and the Alchemical Fire to Recode your Future

This course dives into the essence of Alchemy, the “Chemistry of God,” using the divine spectrum and the activation of the fire element to promote personal healing, spiritual defense and the conscious co-creation of your reality.

All of this is anchored in the Higher Law and Divine Justice, operating with respect for your free will and self-responsibility.

What you will learn

The curriculum involves the knowledge of the manifestation of the divine in matter, and how to do it through the right symbols, elements and verbs.

  • Fundamentals of Sacred Geometry: The study of the primordial forms and measures that project the mystery of creation.
  • The 7 Primordial Vibrational Matrices: How to activate the forces of Faith, Love, Knowledge, Justice, Law, Evolution and Generation.
  • Luminal Activation Procedures: The practical use of candles, consecration of elements and geometric designs on the floor or in alchemical spaces.
  • Psychic Defense and Mirroring: Techniques to filter harmful energies and protect against energetic attacks.
  • Total Purification: A complete protocol to remove miasmas, implants, chips and limiting contracts from past lives.
  • Advanced Ritual Practice: The steadiness of a Guardian Angel, the use of Alchemy to balance others and the combination of symbols to reach your goals in every area of life.
